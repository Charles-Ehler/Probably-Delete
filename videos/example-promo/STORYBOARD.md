## Video direction

Silent, typographic, two-color risograph (cobalt-grid). The permanent graph-paper
grid and top/bottom hairlines persist across all three frames so the cuts read as
one page turning. Rhythm: quick hook, a denser ledger beat, then a held, quiet close.
Every reveal lands on a beat; no exits except the final settle.

Open on the problem every writer has: you need a URL that is safe to print.

Scene 3 (2.2–3.0s): a small QR-block patch pops into the lower-right corner; hold the read.
